The problem

Clean water is lost in three ways at once.

☠️

Undetected pollution

Lab testing is slow and costly, so contaminated water is often used or released before anyone knows.

🌧️

Wasted rainfall

Rain runs off roofs into drains and floods, then communities face shortages in dry months.

🚿

Throwaway wastewater

Bath, laundry and kitchen water (greywater) is drained although it can safely flush toilets and irrigate gardens after treatment.

Our solution: one system, three loops

Built from cheap, easily available parts and designed for homes, schools, apartments and villages.

1

Detect

An ESP32 reads pH, turbidity and TDS sensors every few seconds, classifies the water, and sends alerts to a dashboard or phone.

2

Collect

Roof runoff passes a first-flush diverter that discards the dirty first rain, then fills a tank monitored by a level sensor.

3

Recycle

Greywater goes through screen, sand and carbon filtration, then UV disinfection. A sensor check decides if it is released for reuse or sent back for re-treatment.

    Reuse decision

    The ESP32 checks the treated water. If pH, turbidity and TDS are within limits, a relay opens the valve to the reuse tank (toilet flushing, gardening, cleaning, construction). Otherwise it sends the water back through the filters and raises an alert.

    Treated greywater is for non-drinking use unless it is further purified and lab-verified.
